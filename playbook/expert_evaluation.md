# Who I Would NOT Recommend Following and Why

## Evaluation Criteria

This evaluation is not intended to determine whether an expert is "good" or "bad." Every expert included in this research has made meaningful contributions to B2B SaaS growth and LinkedIn content strategy.

Instead, this section evaluates how well each expert aligns with the objective of this playbook: building a repeatable, evidence-based LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy for B2B SaaS.

The evaluation considers the following criteria:

* Relevance to the scope of this playbook
* Practical applicability for most B2B SaaS organizations
* Consistency of recommendations
* Ability to build foundational understanding
* Long-term strategic value

---

## Expert 1: Todd Clouser

### Why I Would Not Recommend Starting Here

Todd Clouser promotes a creative approach that combines education with entertainment to make B2B content more engaging. His perspective offers valuable ideas for improving audience attention and content quality.

However, I would not recommend Todd Clouser as the primary learning resource for someone building a foundational understanding of LinkedIn Organic Strategy.

Many of his recommendations focus on increasing engagement after a content strategy already exists. They assume the creator has already identified the correct audience, positioning, messaging, and demand generation strategy.

For beginners, these fundamentals should be established before investing significant effort into making content more entertaining.

### Strengths

* Demonstrates how creativity can improve B2B communication.
* Encourages engaging educational content instead of traditional corporate messaging.
* Shows practical ways to capture and retain audience attention.

### Limitations

Entertainment amplifies strong positioning, but it cannot replace it.

Without first understanding customer problems, positioning, messaging, and demand generation, entertaining content may generate attention without producing qualified business outcomes.

### When I Would Recommend Following Todd Clouser

After building a solid strategic foundation, Todd Clouser becomes an excellent resource for improving storytelling, presentation, and audience engagement.

---

## Expert 2: Guillaume Moubeche

### Why I Would Not Recommend Starting Here

Guillaume Moubeche is widely recognized for successfully scaling Lemlist through transparency and Build in Public strategies.

His content demonstrates how sharing company experiences can strengthen trust, credibility, and audience growth.

However, I would not recommend using Build in Public as the default strategy for every B2B SaaS organization.

Many companies operate under confidentiality agreements, enterprise contracts, regulated industries, or highly competitive markets where publicly sharing internal processes is either impossible or strategically inappropriate.

### Strengths

* Demonstrates authentic founder-led communication.
* Provides practical examples of transparent company building.
* Shows how consistent execution can become a powerful marketing strategy.

### Limitations

The success of Build in Public depends heavily on business context.

Organizations handling confidential customer information, regulated industries, or enterprise clients may not have the flexibility to publicly share experiments, failures, or product development decisions.

### When I Would Recommend Following Guillaume Moubeche

For startups, founder-led businesses, and early-stage SaaS companies seeking to build trust through transparency, Guillaume Moubeche remains one of the strongest practitioners to study.

# Final Reflection

The purpose of this evaluation is not to rank experts by quality, but to determine how well their approaches align with the objectives of this playbook.

Every expert included in this repository contributed valuable insights that shaped the final recommendations. However, no single expert provides a complete operating model for LinkedIn Organic Strategy.

The strongest approach is not to follow one authority blindly, but to critically compare multiple perspectives, understand the context behind each recommendation, and synthesize the ideas that best fit a specific business environment.

This philosophy ultimately reflects the core principle of the Continuous Research & Development Loop (CRDL):

> Every solution is temporary until new evidence proves a better one.
