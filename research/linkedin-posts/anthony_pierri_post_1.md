        # Post Content

        I've helped position over 500 companies, and have boiled it down to three core strategies that anyone can learn in ten minutes. 

The concept is simple: 
People love putting tools in buckets. 

You can put your tool in a bucket that everyone knows...
You can put your tool in an bucket that only a small group of people know...
Or you can say my tool is in a brand new bucket that I invented.

The downstream impacts of these three strategies are HUGE. 

They essentially determine how you'll go-to-market, what you compete with, and where your differentiation lives.
