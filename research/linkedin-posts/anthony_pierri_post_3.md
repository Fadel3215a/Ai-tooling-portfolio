        I am convinced that the best product people did not come up in tech. 

Most PMs who have only worked in software are incredibly soft. 

They've only known an industry artificially inflated by venture capital.

This coddled environment does not foster the grit needed to thrive in a startup environment. 

Jamie is a good friend of mine. She started in the restaurant business (an absolutely grueling industry) and worked her way up from line cook to executive chef.

She then learned UX research & design on nights and weekends to make a huge pivot into tech. Now, she's making the jump into product management, and has already built multiple  products on her own with AI. 

If you need a DOER, someone with agency who will get shit done without you needing to babysit, someone who will take initiative BEFORE you ask them to... then hire her. 

Looking for any PM or associate PM roles you've got open.
