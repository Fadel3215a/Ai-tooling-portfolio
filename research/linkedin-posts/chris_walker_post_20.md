        # Post Content

        Imagine trying to tell Gary the factory worker that he should learn to use a computer and make money with his brain. It's 1975. His plant just started implementing industrial automation.

He's been working there for 22 years. Good wage. 20 more years and he retires with a pension.

He can see the headcount shrinking. Some of his friends at other plants already got laid off.

So he's working harder than ever. Coming in early. Staying late. Learning every new machine they bring in.

But skilled physical labor was being automated. The premium moved up a level to the people who could think, analyze, strategize, and manage. Knowledge work commanded a value that physical labor no longer could.

Four years later, Gary was laid off and struggled to find his footing in the new economy.

__

Now consider that many people reading this are Gary.

They've spent 5-20 years building a career as a knowledge worker. Making good money. Respected in their field.

Their company started implementing AI in 2023.

They can see their team shrinking. Friends at other companies are getting laid off.

So they're working harder than ever. Starting early. Available late. Learning every new AI tool they can find.

But knowledge work is being automated. The premium is moving up a level again to the people who have developed what AI cannot replicate. Clarity. Self-trust. Emotional Sovereignty. Judgment. Creativity. Deep Human Connection. Presence. Adaptability.

The shift isn't about working harder or learning more tools to do knowledge work better.

It's about elevating to the next level. Again. 

The premium skills of the future are built in your nervous system, not in your mind.

__

And I get it. Saying this to a knowledge worker right now probably sounds just as foreign as someone telling Gary to learn how to use a computer and make money with his brain in 1975.
