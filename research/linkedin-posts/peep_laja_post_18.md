        # Post Content

        When B2B vendors look the same, buyers go looking for the difference. And they get frustrated when they can't find it.

We asked 100 marketing leaders what they do when the shortlist feels interchangeable. 

The #1 move, 41%, is to push for a trial or demo. They want to see the difference with their own eyes, because the website never spelled it out.

Here's one VP who told us why she picked one tool over two rivals: "their AI use cases were clearly stronger. Drift felt legacy with 'AI' bolted on, and Chili Piper's use cases were super broad."

That's the level of differentiation buyers want. Named, compared, concrete.

But almost no website talks like that. Most vendors hedge. They won't name the competitor. They won't take the sharp position. So they default to "AI-powered, automate your workflow, close more deals", and disappear into the sea of sameness.

Your buyers aren't asking you to be louder. They're asking you to be specific about who you beat and why.

Say the thing your competitor can't.
