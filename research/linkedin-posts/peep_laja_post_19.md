        # Post Content

        87% of B2B buyers get to a vendor through a peer recommendation.

72% already knew the vendor before the search started.

"Their messaging stood out"? 29%. Fourth place.

We used Wynter to ask 100 marketing leaders what actually gets a vendor onto their shortlist.

The thing that gets you considered is other people talking about you, and having been around long enough to be familiar.

So the questions worth asking: how many of your customers are happy enough to put their name on a recommendation? How easy do you make it for them to do it? And are you consistent enough, long enough, to be the vendor people already know when the search begins?

You get shortlisted by being the one people already trust.
