        5 Steps you need to take when you want to leverage LinkedIn to generate leads.
Step 1: Buy a Lead List.

Step 2: Auto-engage with their content.
Step 3: Copy-paste Outreach to all leads.
Step 4: Have an automated follow up message.
Step 5: Not responding? Keep trying until they give in.

That's it! You might land 1 or 2 clients.
In the meantime you'll ruin the trust and credibility with 99% of your network.
Do I have your attention now? 

Then let's talk real business and sustainable sales strategy
Below my TRUST method, used with + 300 B2B sales teams

Step 1: Target
↳ Use a multiple layer outreach structure 
↳ Think company level and individual level
↳ Use Sales Navigator, a CRM or an integrated system

Step 2: Reach
↳ Engage with your target audience (leave meaningful comments)
↳ Do this 2 or 3 times on the same account
↳ Not publishing? Visit their profile, trigger the algorithm

Step 3: Unlock
↳ Received a reaction to your comment?
↳ They viewed your profile as well?
↳ Follow up with a connection request. It's about them, not about you

Step 4: Strengthen
↳ Forgotten by +90% of B2B Sellers, build trust before you try to convert
↳ Have a "magical offer", something unique in value
↳ Don't send it, ask for consent. Try to go email

Step 5:
↳ Positive reply? Willing to receive your content?
↳ Send it asap and ask for feedback?
↳ Follow up, offer more insights in a 15-minute introduction call

BAM! That's your first meeting
